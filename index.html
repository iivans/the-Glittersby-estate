THE NEW YORK EVENING REGISTER

Sunday, September 6, 1923 — Price 3¢ — New York City — All the news that's fit to print

🎩 SOCIETY — GLITTERSBY ESTATE TO HOST SOCIETY'S MOST TALKED-ABOUT EVENING

The doors of the magnificent Glittersby Estate will open tonight for what promises to be one of the season's most exclusive gatherings. Society's finest names are expected to attend the private affair, hosted by the mysterious and notoriously wealthy Mr. Glittersby.

Guests have been instructed to arrive in their finest 1920s attire. Dinner, dancing, live jazz, and an evening of games are expected. Rumors of a surprise announcement have circulated throughout Manhattan, though the estate's representatives have refused to confirm anything. One thing is certain: tonight, everyone will be watching everyone else.

🎩 SOCIETY NOTES — WHO'S WHO AT THE ESTATE

Several notable figures are expected to attend tonight's gathering, among them business magnates, performers, athletes, socialites, and other prominent members of New York society. A source close to the estate described the guest list as "a rather dangerous collection of people in one very large house." The source declined to elaborate.

🚔 POLICE BLOTTER — MYSTERIOUS INCIDENT ON FIFTH AVENUE

Police responded late Saturday evening to reports of a disturbance near Fifth Avenue. Witnesses reported hearing raised voices before a motorcar was seen leaving the area at considerable speed. No arrests have been announced. Police declined to comment on whether the incident was connected to tonight's Glittersby gathering.

🥃 PROHIBITION — BUSINESS IS BOOMING

Despite the continued efforts of federal authorities, New York's underground liquor trade shows no signs of slowing. Bootleggers continue to find creative ways to move spirits throughout the city. The latest concoction, dubbed the "Bee's Knees," has become the drink of choice among New York's elite. One police officer, speaking anonymously, remarked: "If you know where to look, you'll find a bottle." Authorities deny that any illegal liquor will be served at tonight's estate gathering. Nobody believes them.

🎾 SPORTS — A BUSY WEEKEND IN NEW YORK

Baseball fans have filled the city's ballparks this weekend, while boxing and tennis continue to attract New York's sporting elite. Tennis champion Imotta Bounds has reportedly been challenging nearly everyone she meets to a match; friends say she "doesn't know how to refuse a competition." A stray tennis ball was discovered in the manor garden this afternoon.

🎬 ENTERTAINMENT — ROXY THEATRE PACKED

New York's theaters remain crowded this weekend as audiences continue to flock toward the latest silent-film attractions, and the Roxy Theatre is expected to draw another large crowd tonight. Meanwhile, rumors continue to circulate regarding a certain film star attending a private gathering uptown. A ticket stub from the Roxy Theatre was found near the estate entrance. Piano player Avery Keyes will perform live jazz throughout the evening. "I see everything in this estate," Keyes remarked with a wink.

☁️ WEATHER — CLEAR SKIES, PERFECT FOR AN EVENING OUT

Today: clear skies with mild temperatures. Tonight: cool and clear — ideal conditions for New Yorkers attending tonight's private parties and society gatherings. The weather vane on the manor roof appears to be stuck.

❓ SOCIETY'S QUESTION OF THE DAY

What secret will be revealed tonight at the Glittersby Estate? Whatever happens, our society correspondent will be watching. — The New York Evening Register
